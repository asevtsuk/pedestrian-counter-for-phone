Counting period

The count stops itself when the period runs out. Per-hour figures are extrapolated from the observed window, so treat short periods as indicative only.

Snapshots

A 640 px JPEG of the same cropped view the detector sees. One at the start, then only when the phone moves beyond the distance above or the scene itself changes. Lower the sensitivity number to trigger more readily; raise it if passing vehicles set it off.

Detection

People are located by the midpoint of the bottom edge of their box — where their feet meet the ground. That point is what crosses the gate and enters the zone.

Validate before you trust it: hand-count two minutes of the same view and compare. Accuracy falls off with distance, groups walking abreast, and low light.
